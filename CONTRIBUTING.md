# Contributions

KaSiHub is a closed, proprietary product. It is not an open-source or public
contribution project, and unsolicited code, pull requests, designs, documents,
assets, or other contributions are not accepted.

Only contributors who have prior written authorisation from the rights holder
and an applicable signed employment, contractor, confidentiality, intellectual
property, or contribution agreement may submit material. Repository access or
the technical ability to open a pull request is not authorisation.

Do not submit confidential information, third-party material, personal data,
secrets, or material you do not have the legal right to provide.
